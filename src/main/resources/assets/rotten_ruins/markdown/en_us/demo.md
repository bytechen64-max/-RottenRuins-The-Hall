## Before departure

1. Check the map.
   - Mark the entrance and the nearest exit.
   - Note where the two routes meet.
2. Check your wand.

- [x] Record the surface coordinates.
- [ ] Confirm the exit route.

### Recording coordinates

Keep the `entrance bearing` and the `rally point` in the same entry. Copy the fields below into your own notes.

```text
Entrance bearing: north
Rally point: stone pillar outside the entrance
Exit route:
    Upper corridor → east exit
```
